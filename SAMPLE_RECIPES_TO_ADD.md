## 🍳 BREAKFAST RECIPES

### 1. Classic Oatmeal Bowl
**Recipe Name:** Classic Oatmeal Bowl  
**Description:** Healthy and filling oatmeal with fruits and nuts  
**Prep Time:** 5  
**Cook Time:** 10  
**Servings:** 1  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Calories:** 350  
**Protein:** 12  
**Carbs:** 55  
**Fats:** 8  
**Dietary Tags:** vegetarian  
**Instructions:**
```
1. Bring 1 cup of water to boil in a small pot
2. Add 1/2 cup rolled oats and reduce heat to medium
3. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally
4. Remove from heat and let sit for 2 minutes
5. Top with sliced banana, berries, and a drizzle of honey
6. Sprinkle with chopped almonds
7. Serve warm
```
**Ingredients:**
- Rolled Oats: 50g
- Banana: 100g
- Almonds: 10g
- Honey: 15ml

### 2. Scrambled Eggs with Toast
**Recipe Name:** Scrambled Eggs with Toast  
**Description:** Protein-rich breakfast with fluffy scrambled eggs  
**Prep Time:** 5  
**Cook Time:** 8  
**Servings:** 1  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Calories:** 380  
**Protein:** 22  
**Carbs:** 35  
**Fats:** 15  
**Dietary Tags:** vegetarian  
**Instructions:**
```
1. Crack 2 eggs into a bowl and whisk with a fork
2. Add a splash of milk, salt, and pepper
3. Heat butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat
4. Pour in the egg mixture
5. Gently stir with a spatula until eggs are cooked but still soft
6. Toast 2 slices of whole wheat bread
7. Serve eggs on toast with a side of cherry tomatoes
```
**Ingredients:**
- Eggs: 100g (2 eggs)
- Whole Wheat Bread: 60g (2 slices)
- Butter: 10g
- Milk: 30ml
- Cherry Tomatoes: 50g

## 🍛 LUNCH RECIPES

### 4. Grilled Chicken Salad
**Recipe Name:** Grilled Chicken Salad  
**Description:** Healthy protein-packed salad with grilled chicken  
**Prep Time:** 15  
**Cook Time:** 15  
**Servings:** 1  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Calories:** 420  
**Protein:** 38  
**Carbs:** 25  
**Fats:** 18  
**Dietary Tags:** high-protein  
**Instructions:**
```
1. Season chicken breast with salt, pepper, and olive oil
2. Grill chicken for 6-7 minutes per side until cooked through
3. Let chicken rest for 5 minutes, then slice
4. In a large bowl, combine mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber
5. Add sliced chicken on top
6. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice
7. Toss gently and serve
```
**Ingredients:**
- Chicken Breast: 150g
- Mixed Greens: 100g
- Cherry Tomatoes: 80g
- Cucumber: 50g
- Olive Oil: 15ml
- Lemon: 20g

### 5. Vegetable Stir Fry with Rice
**Recipe Name:** Vegetable Stir Fry with Rice  
**Description:** Colorful mixed vegetables with brown rice  
**Prep Time:** 10  
**Cook Time:** 20  
**Servings:** 1  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Calories:** 450

**Fats:** 10  
**Dietary Tags:** vegan, vegetarian

### 6. Chickpea Curry
**Recipe Name:** Chickpea Curry  
**Description:** Spicy and flavorful Indian-style chickpea curry  
**Prep Time:** 10  
**Cook Time:** 25  
**Servings:** 2  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Calories:** 380  
**Protein:** 15  
**Carbs:** 55  
**Fats:** 12  
**Dietary Tags:** vegan, vegetarian  
**Instructions:**
```
1. Heat oil in a pot, add cumin seeds until they sizzle
2. Add chopped onions and cook until golden
3. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook for 1 minute
4. Add tomatoes, turmeric, chili powder, and salt
5. Cook until tomatoes are soft
6. Add chickpeas and 1 cup water
7. Simmer for 15 minutes
8. Garnish with cilantro and serve with rice or naan
```
**Ingredients:**
- Chickpeas: 200g (cooked)
- Onions: 100g
- Tomatoes: 150g
- Garlic: 10g
- Ginger: 10g
- Cumin Seeds: 5g
- Turmeric: 3g
- Vegetable Oil: 15ml

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Calories:** 480

**Carbs:** 30  
**Fats:** 22  
**Dietary Tags:** high-protein, pescatarian

## 📝 NOTES

### Dietary Tags Available:
- `vegetarian` - No meat or fish
- `vegan` - No animal products
- `high-protein` - High in protein content
- `low-carb` - Low in carbohydrates
- `gluten-free` - No gluten
- `dairy-free` - No dairy products
- `pescatarian` - Fish but no meat

### Tips for Adding Recipes:
1. **Be specific with measurements** - Use grams (g) or milliliters (ml)
2. **Break down instructions** - One step per line
3. **Include all ingredients** - Make sure they exist in your ingredients database
4. **Set realistic times** - Prep and cook times in minutes
5. **Add dietary tags** - Helps with meal plan generation
6. **Calculate nutrition** - Approximate values are fine

### Common Ingredient Units:
- Solid foods: grams (g)
- Liquids: milliliters (ml)
- Can also use: kg, l, cups, tbsp, tsp, pcs (pieces)
